    Brands gaining and losing

    Buying rules after the April 2026 excise reform

    • Buy left-hand-drive cars only. Right-hand-drive cars pay 3x excise (RHD electric: flat GEL 3,000).
    • Petrol, diesel and hybrid cars should be 6 years old or younger at customs clearance: GEL 1.5 per cm³ instead of GEL 4.5.
    • Hybrids 6 years or younger keep a 60% excise discount (about GEL 1,200 on a 2.0 L car).
    • Electric cars pay no excise at any age.
    • The age cutoff seems to move one model year each January: clear older stock before the new year.
    • Excise is refunded if a car is re-exported within 3 months of clearance.

    Already taken: official dealers in Georgia

    These brands already have an exclusive or official distributor, so a new dealer would have to work under them.

    How to become an official distributor

    • Small markets like Georgia usually get one exclusive national distributor. Brands prefer groups that already run dealerships.
    • Typical steps (BYD's published process): application with company profile and business plan → online presentation → site visit → letter of intent → showroom build and staff training → dealer agreement.
    • Expect to show strong finances, a showroom and service workshop to brand standards, a parts stock and a first order of cars. Brands do not publish minimum amounts.
    • Rough timeline (industry practice, not a published figure): 3–9 months to a letter of intent, 6–12 months to opening.
    • Ask whether the seat is free first: Chery's dealer may hold Omoda/Jaecoo and Exeed, Changan's dealer may hold Avatr, the Geely/Zeekr dealer may claim Lynk & Co, the BYD dealer will likely get Denza.
    • Brands often hand Georgia to a Caucasus group (Hongqi went to an Armenian distributor). Talking to the Armenian or Azerbaijani importer can be a shortcut.
    • Since April 2026 electric cars pay no excise and new hybrids get 60% off: plug-in, range-extender and electric line-ups fit best.
    • Risk: Georgia bans re-exporting US/EU-made cars to Russia and Belarus. Brands and customs watch distributors whose volumes are far above local demand.

    Data sources

    • Geostat vehicle statistics (automobile.geostat.ge): first registrations by brand, model, fuel, body, engine, age, colour and region; vehicle fleet; vehicle imports new vs used; fuel prices. Quarterly.
    • UN Comtrade (comtradeapi.un.org): Georgia's customs figures as reported by Geostat (car imports and re-exports by fuel/engine type and country, monthly), plus world car exports and Georgia's re-export markets, yearly.
    • Geostat statistical database (pc-axis.geostat.ge): GDP growth, inflation, average salary.
    • National Bank of Georgia (nbg.gov.ge): official exchange rates and policy rate.
    • World Bank: yearly indicators for Georgia.
    • Eurostat: EU new-car registrations by fuel.
    • Excise rules: Georgian Tax Code as amended in April 2026 (fixed in the app).
    • Import a brand: Georgian registrations per brand are live from Geostat; global sales, official dealers in Georgia and nearby countries, and how to apply come from company reports and news researched in November 2026 (fixed in the app).
